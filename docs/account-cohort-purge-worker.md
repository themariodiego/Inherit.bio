# Owned cohort deletion worker prerequisite

This stream implements the owned-cohort request, affected-recipient notice/cancellation envelope and due worker storage/runtime cleanup for published `true_two_parent` and `anonymous_donor` cohorts. A request commits every exact current recipient notice or rolls back its nonce and hold. A due claim requires the original saved notice binding; legacy unnotified requests remain refused. Production ingest remains disabled, and full database, browser and provider acceptance remain unverified until the integrator completes them.

The ownership selector comes from `docs/retention.md` (`account-deletion.purge-owned`) and `docs/route-register.json`, `lifecycleDispositionContracts.account-deletion-v1.deadlineSelection.cohorts.ownedCohort`. It does not depend on another parent’s live consent. Current subject and embryo cohort joins select parent-controlled sources; the historical cohort UUID in a claimed source never selects it. Already-detached custody, canonical source, membership, part and genotype rows remain byte-identical.

The parent’s ingest session and full worker job carry account/principal authority and are deleted. Their original IDs survive only in `private.claimed_embryo_ingest_receipts` and `private.claimed_embryo_job_receipts`. The immutable receipts carry the exact historical cohort, ingest/publication/attempt revisions and dispatch/manifest/hash/build bindings. They have no parent account/principal/contact/credential FK and no copied full job payload, raw object handle or key. Closed deferred guards require every retained canonical part to match either its live production dispatch tuple or its exact already-detached claimed custody tuple and both receipts. API roles cannot create receipts directly.

`account_embryo_unwinds_v1` is a service-only read under one exact unexpired deletion claim. The retention endpoint disposes at most one selected unwind and 25 objects per account invocation, using the existing provider acknowledgement doors. It requires an empty durable reread before account Storage completion. Failed, canceled, uncertain or still-pending provider work blocks account Storage completion, database purge and Auth deletion. Retrying never renews the original account deadline. Documentary bases, unresolved claims, unfinished ingest and draft-only graphs remain refused because their private-document/terminal workflows are outside this prerequisite.

The SQL proof uses synthetic publication through the actual R2 reserve/ACK doors and the actual approved custody transition. Its provider acknowledgements are metadata-only fixtures targeting the SQL verifier; crossed receipts and false empty-tombstone digests refuse without marking any object disposed. It does not delete Storage rows or change their deletion guard. Hosted-provider erasure and the full browser/request/notice journey are not claimed. The exact purge inventory is 150 stores; the two retained provenance receipts have deferred claimant export readers, and the temporary worker plan is explicitly excluded as internal control state. No gate or route reachability entry becomes YES.

The notice follow-on must implement `account-deletion-v1.notifiedPrincipals`, `noticeTransaction` and `cancellation` in `docs/route-register.json` (lines 7724–7741 at this parent). Resolve the exact current `notice_recipients` through `private.embryo_cohort_set_v1` under the cohort’s basis, participant and recipient revisions; also include any separate current identified-donor attribution recipient. A deleting holder’s notice cannot substitute for another recipient’s notice. Missing or ambiguous current contacts must roll back the request, not silently omit a required recipient. Cancellation must send deduplicated notices to the same applicable affected envelope while preserving independently ended consent/resources and the original precreated phase deadlines.

The public nonce wrappers are `request_account_deletion_v2` and `cancel_account_deletion_v2` in `20260930140200_account_operation_nonce_rendered.sql`. They retain the one-time nonce transaction and delegate to the v1 internal bodies from `20260901033000_account_deletion_notice_runtime.sql`; the v1 worker grant retirement must stay intact. The current `account-deletion-notice` template says “Your Inherit account deletion is scheduled” and carries holder-only `noticeEndsAt`, `cancelPath` and `exportPath` inputs. A new registered affected-recipient template, payload validator, email renderer and send callback must be added deliberately; do not reuse holder-specific cancellation/export links. The existing principal mail helper accepts principal, template/purpose, target kind/ID, payload, deduplication key, expiry and optional token purpose/target. Its historical newest-contact fallback is not sufficient evidence for a mandatory notice’s unique current contact. The latest general delivery door is restated in `20260930232000_embryo_parent_withdrawal.sql`; recheck provider attempt identity and authority at both claim and submission.


The follow-on migration `20260930238000_account_cohort_notices.sql` stores the exact cohort revisions and each original principal/contact/authority/outbox tuple in the existing retention phase's `affectedNotice` envelope. It introduces no new table, deadline or generic recipient API. Missing, stale or ambiguous current contacts refuse. Request and cancellation use separate registered token-free templates with only their event timestamp. The ordinary mail worker's exact principal/contact revision and attempt checks still apply immediately before provider submission. Cancellation re-resolves the applicable current envelope and requires every recipient to be part of the original notice envelope, preserves independently ended consents/resources, cancels only unstarted account phases, and revokes all old holder auth sessions. The holder must sign in normally again; no session is silently restored.

The collector includes separate current identified donor attribution only with its exact current signature and attribution revision. This does not implement the absent identified-donor publication/evidence producer or widen the worker's two supported bases. Unsupported documentary bases, draft-only/unfinished ingest and unresolved claim graphs remain refused. Broad foreign sharing, claimed-bound owner deletion and non-owned role-specific cohort effects remain outside this supported graph. No full project acceptance or route proof is inferred from the synthetic SQL or component tests.
