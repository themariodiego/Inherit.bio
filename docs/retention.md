# Inherit retention and disposition register

Status: binding
Baseline application SHA: `864736979c92a08ba77e8580d61946eba6864918`

This is the sole authority for retention clocks, deletion clocks, notice clocks and purge targets. Routes and jobs reference the stable IDs below and do not repeat their numbers. A later legal hold may delay physical deletion only where a committed, reviewed legal artifact names the basis and maximum; it never restores product access or permits new analysis.

## Enforcement

The retention job computes targets by immutable database identifiers and joins, covers both canonical and legacy Storage layouts, and is idempotent. Acceptance re-queries every named table and object with the service role after the job and requires zero prohibited rows or objects. Production release is blocked if any route, UI string, migration default, job schedule or legal artifact states a different clock.
