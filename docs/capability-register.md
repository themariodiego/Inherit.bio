# Capability register

Status: binding (brief X15, X16.6, G7.4). Authority for the ship/withhold
status of every capability in Inherit's service category. One row per
capability; silence about a capability is never a withholding.

A row carries one of three terminal statuses:

- `shipped` — a person can reach it end to end on the hosted service.
- `shipped-degraded` — it ships in a narrower form and the row names the
  honest UI state that tells the person so. A capability that works end to
  end under TEST-LOCAL, but is off in production because no real
  jurisdiction permits it, is `shipped-degraded` too (owner decision,
  `docs/protocol/decisions.md`, 16 October 2026; brief line 2580). Its
  UI-state cell opens with **Production: `jurisdiction-unavailable`**, names
  the guarding capability, quotes the copy production users see and cites
  the browser test that proves it; it then says what TEST-LOCAL delivers.
  A capability that works end to end under TEST-LOCAL with a synthetic
  reviewed release, but that production keeps unavailable until the owner
  approves the starter list, the guarded import runs and a named reviewer
  activates each condition, is `shipped-degraded` too (owner decision,
  `docs/protocol/decisions.md`, 22 October 2026, G7.4). Its UI-state cell
  opens with **Production: unavailable until reviewed activation.**, quotes
  the sentence production users see and cites the browser test against the
  build with the TEST-LOCAL flag unset that pins it; it then says what
  TEST-LOCAL delivers. Today that is the two carrier rows.
  A capability that the owner decided to build and test on synthetic data,
  that works end to end under TEST-LOCAL, and that production keeps refusing
  exactly as before, is `shipped-degraded` too (owner decision,
  `docs/protocol/decisions.md`, 16 October 2026, "BAM/CRAM read calling and
  the Neanderthal estimate (G7.4)"). Its UI-state cell opens with
  **Production: refused before any upload session.**, quotes the sentence
  production users see and cites the browser test against the build with the
  TEST-LOCAL flag unset that pins it; it then says what TEST-LOCAL delivers.
  Today that is the BAM/CRAM row.
- `withheld` — withheld on evidenced impossibility; the row references the
  dossier under `docs/withheld/<capability>.md`.

A row that has not reached a terminal status carries `not shipped` and names
the surface a person meets today and the workstream that owns it. `not
shipped` is not a withholding: no dossier exists for it and nothing in the
product says it is impossible. G7.4 stays NO while any row carries it.

Counts on this revision: shipped 7 · shipped-degraded 19 · withheld 0 ·
not shipped 0. They are counted from the table below and must agree with it.
Withheld capabilities: 0. Pharmacogenomics left `withheld` on 2026-09-03 under
ADR 0021; its dossier `docs/withheld/pharmacogenomics.md` remains as the record
of why nothing more ships. `docs/withheld/` holds no other dossier, so no other
row may carry `withheld`.

Corrected on 2026-09-09 against the code, not against the previous revision:

## Sequence ingest (BAM, CRAM) reclassified — 22 October 2026

The owner decided to build BAM/CRAM read calling rather than withhold it, and
to test it on synthetic data (`docs/protocol/decisions.md`, "2026-10-16 —
BAM/CRAM read calling and the Neanderthal estimate (G7.4)"). The capability
stays TEST-LOCAL only, and production keeps today's behaviour. ADR 0038
records the design, and `ownReadCallingPreflight` in
`policyContracts.genetic-file-ingest-v1` (`docs/route-register.json`) records
the contract.

What was built. Under TEST-LOCAL, the own-DNA picker can take a BAM, or a
CRAM chosen together with its reference FASTA. The browser reads the file on
the device (`src/lib/genome/reads/`) and calls genotypes at the 2,024 GRCh38
positions the product reads (`data/ref/read-calling-loci.json`: 145 report,
168 ancestry and 1,716 carrier positions). It uploads only the single-sample
VCF that results, through the unchanged VCF path. The reads and the
reference are never sent or stored. The canonical observed calls are the VCF
path's own, so every downstream surface reads them unchanged. A position no
read covers is not listed and shows "not covered". Too few or unclear reads
give `./.` and show the no-call sentence. The thresholds are engineering
defaults pending scientific review (`src/lib/genome/reads/thresholds.ts`). The
VCF header, the canonical provenance and the report source facts all say so.

One migration was added, approved by the integrating session and labelled
PRODUCTION RELEASE: GUARDED: `20261022110000_read_called_input_provenance.sql`.
It is an md5-guarded patch of `private.read_own_input_sources_v1` that
projects the TEST-LOCAL `readCalls` provenance in its closed shape. Ordinary
sources are byte-identical (`supabase/tests/read_called_input_provenance.sql`).

What production shows. Production is unchanged. The picker offers one file
and states "Inherit does not accept BAM, CRAM or FASTQ files. Ask your lab
for a VCF. Changing the file name does not change its format." A readable
synthetic BAM or CRAM is refused with the unrecognised-file sentence before
any upload session is issued (`e2e/read-calls.nojurisdiction.spec.ts`). The
server still refuses a BAM or CRAM declaration, and cleans up a misdeclared
upload (`e2e/tier2-upload.spec.ts`, unchanged). No production deployment
records read-calling provenance.

This is not scientific review, validation on real laboratory files, or
clinical activation, and it closes none of the open work in the row. Counts
move from shipped-degraded 17 and not shipped 2 to shipped-degraded 18 and
not shipped 1. Neanderthal ancestry was still `not shipped` on the BAM/CRAM
branch; the next section moves it.

### Honest absence and the dossier rule

The absence reasons are short and specific: the BAM/CRAM provider message
and the own chooser's `OWN_UPLOAD_COPY.sequenceReadsUnavailable` direct the
person to a VCF (Neanderthal ancestry left this list on 2026-10-22, now
`shipped-degraded`; see the dated section above); `NO_CLASSIFIED_POSITIONS` says that no negative
carrier screen follows; `INGEST_UNAVAILABLE_SENTENCE` states that this site
cannot take embryo files; `NO_RESULTS_SENTENCE` and `NO_ROWS_SENTENCE` name
the empty embryo model registry while preserving the real quality check.
Each reason is at most 40 words. None promises an externally controlled
approval. Quality's missing-method and missing-source statements describe
only the measurement the current file cannot supply.

No scoped dossier or three actually built, materially different failed
designs was found for any of these seven capabilities. The pharmacogenomics
dossier and its three verbatim design failures remain historical evidence
for that separate capability; they are not seven reusable failures. A
refused input, a synthetic negative test or an unrun design is not a failed
alternative implementation.

If withholding is later proposed for any one of these seven, its own dossier
must supply all nine elements from the brief: (1) the exact capability;
(2) a legal, scientific, data-availability or safety obstacle; (3) primary
source evidence and access date; (4) three materially different designs
actually built, with each named gate failure; (5) the narrowest honest subset
and its delivery evidence; (6) a testable change that would remove the
obstacle; (7) the actual reason of at most 40 words, with no “soon”, “yet” or
“currently” when dependent on external reform, decision or outcome evidence;
(8) an ADR; and (9) its own `withheld` register entry. An external gate blocker
also needs the exact command, verbatim output and three attempted approach
families. Missing implementation or an outstanding review satisfies neither
route. The status counts, all seven `not shipped` rows and G7.4 remain as
recorded until their substantive release requirements are met.
