# Owner review work order

Date: 2 October 2026. Owner setup and first review target: 5 October 2026.

## Carrier conditions

Start with `docs/carrier-condition-reviews/CFTR.md`. Read the condition,
source links, finding text and known limits. Complete its review checklist
yourself. Keep the activation choice separate from preparation of the pack.
Codex will not fill your signature, role, severity judgement or approval.

CFTR is first. The remaining seven files already exist in
`docs/carrier-condition-reviews/`. Prepare the next condition after your
CFTR review. Preparation alone does not make any result visible.

## Scientific sources

You chose to check the report claims and citations yourself. Use
`data/citations.json` for the source register and
`docs/claim-surface-backlog.json` for the non-template worklist. Read each
original source and record the exact claim it supports. Use a verbatim
supporting quote of 25 words or fewer. Do not approve a claim from a summary
alone. Codex will reconcile already-reviewed items before giving you the
remaining source worklist.

## Legal review

Codex prepares the review pack while you find a lawyer. The first questions
cover the proposed liability wording, consent texts, protective legal
statements and the rules for each country and capability. A pack is not a
legal determination. Each required qualified reviewer must write and sign
their own determination. No real jurisdiction opens from this work order.
