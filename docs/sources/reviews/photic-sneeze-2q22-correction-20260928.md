# Photic sneeze reflex (2q22 report): Wang 2019 citation clarified and dated

Applies to `photic-sneeze-reflex-2q22`. Source review on 28 September 2026;
agent review, not clinical or human signoff.

## Status

This report's rendered prose is correct and was not changed. Its summary and
genotype explanations describe the European-ancestry Eriksson 2010 finding
(C allele linked to more reports), which is a registered canonical claim bound to
that source. No reader-visible wording was corrected, so this report carries no
correction notice and no register entry.

## Why it was reviewed

The report's second citation, [Wang et al., Sci Rep 2019, PMID 30899065](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30899065/),
was flagged during the 28 September citation review because it reports the
opposite allele. PMC full text confirms Wang replicates the association of
rs10427255 at 2q22.3 with the photic sneeze reflex in a Chinese cohort, but with
the minor T allele raising the odds (OR 1.68), whereas Eriksson (European) links
the C allele. See `photic-sneeze-zeb2-correction-20260928.md` for the paired
report where that difference appears in the prose and was corrected.

Wang 2019 is retained here as a replication of the 2q22 locus association, which
is what it is cited for, and is dated now that it has been retrieved and
confirmed. It is not cited for a per-allele direction in this report; the
genotype explanations remain Eriksson-scoped ("in the study"). The
reviewed-content freeze in `src/lib/claims/canonical-report-data.test.ts` is
re-anchored to include the dated citation.

This is a limited citation review and claims no personal prediction.
