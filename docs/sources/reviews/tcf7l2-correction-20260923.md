# TCF7L2 diabetes report correction

Applies only to `type-2-diabetes-tcf7l2-rs7903146`. Sources read on
23 September 2026. This is agent source review, not clinical approval or
human signoff.

[Grant 2006, PMID 16415884](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16415884/),
DOI `10.1038/ng1732`: the complete author abstract was read through the
official Europe PMC core record. The full paper was inaccessible. Its named
marker is DG10S478; its carrier frequencies and relative risks are not
transferred to rs7903146. The canonical quotation reuses the earlier
batch-02 excerpt. This source records the original locus discovery, not
support for the trial-specific paragraphs.

[Florez 2006, PMID 16855264](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16855264/),
DOI `10.1056/NEJMoa062418`: the [primary manuscript HTML](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1762036/)
and an author-hosted publisher PDF were obtained. The methods, results, Table 2, figure captions and discussion
were read, including the limits on heterozygote power. No original participant
data or supplementary study was reviewed. The trial enrolled overweight
people with elevated fasting glucose and impaired glucose tolerance, without
diabetes at entry. Follow-up averaged about three years.

For rs7903146, Table 2 reports CT/CC hazard ratio 1.00 (95% CI 0.84–1.19)
and TT/CC 1.55 (1.20–2.01). CC participants also developed diabetes. The
absence of a detected CT increase in this trial does not establish a general
null association. The discussion notes limited power; the report explicitly
preserves that uncertainty. Genotype–intervention interactions were not
significant. This does not establish equal benefit for each genotype, and
the new text gives no treatment recommendation.

[NCBI VCV000007413.7](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/7413/)
was read only for Variant Details identity: rs7903146 is forward GRCh38
chr10:112998590 C>T. The local excerpt records those fields, the accession
and actual access time. No submitted clinical classification or population
frequency is used. This mapping does not validate a consumer assay.

The four corrected paragraphs remove an unsupported lifetime reassurance,
time-sensitive largest-effect ranking, general population genotype
frequencies, causal-mechanism language and unmeasured dominance of lifestyle
over other risk. They retain the observed group association, narrowed to
the cited trial. DNA calls, allele normalization, missing/no-call handling,
title, evidence tier and every other template field remain unchanged except
the two dated citation records. No personal absolute risk is calculated.

Historical captured templates and outcomes are not rewritten or lent the
new citations. Their exact old text is registered separately for the
historical-correction notice and Copilot refusal. This finite correction does
not establish full-catalog accuracy, hosted deployment or G1.11/G4.7 closure.
