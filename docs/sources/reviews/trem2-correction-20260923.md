# TREM2 R47H report correction

Applies only to `trem2-r47h-alzheimers`. Sources read on 23 September 2026.
This is agent source review, not clinical approval or human signoff.

[Jonsson, PMID 23150908](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23150908/), DOI
`10.1056/NEJMoa1211103`: the complete author abstract and Figure 1 caption
were read through indexed PubMed, plus the opening Methods and Results
passages through the primary PMC article's index. Discovery was Icelandic,
with replication in US, Norwegian, Dutch and German groups. The combined
association odds ratio was 2.90 (95% CI 2.16–3.91). Older unaffected carriers
were observed; this does not establish a reader's lifetime probability.
The opening Results' Icelandic allele frequency is not a general carrier
proportion. The proposed inflammatory mechanism is a hypothesis.

[Guerreiro, PMID 23150934](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23150934/), DOI
`10.1056/NEJMoa1211851`: the author abstract and figure captions were opened
and read. The abstract describes case-control sequencing, imputation and
additional direct genotyping. It reports an R47H association and frames its
conclusion around rare heterozygous TREM2 variants. Its pooled exon-2 counts
are not R47H-only genotype counts. No TT-specific table was read.

Both papers appeared online on 14 November 2012 and in the 10 January 2013
issue. Full papers and tables were inaccessible: primary PMC pages returned
browser challenges and publisher/author-repository PDF requests failed.
This note records author-abstract access and the specified indexed passages,
not a full-paper review. No research-source quotation is added here; the
canonical excerpts use 8 and 17 words respectively.

[NCBI RefSNP](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/snp/rs75932628) was opened and its
Position, Genomic Placements, transcript/protein and HGVS tables read on the
same date. Forward GRCh38 chr6:41161514 C>T maps to the minus-strand
`NM_018965.4:c.140G>A` and `NP_061838.1:p.Arg47His`. GRCh37 is chr6:41129252.
The same rsID also includes C>A/R47L; this report interprets only C/T.
The current NCBI ClinVar allele description agrees. No condition-specific
ClinVar classification is used as an Alzheimer's association verdict.
Opposite-strand file letters still require the existing normalization.

The four corrected strings retain the supported group association. They
remove lifetime reassurance, universal population percentages, a comparison
to APOE, personal weighting of other risk factors and a probable-test-error
judgment. The cited evidence does not establish the imported assay's accuracy.
The TT text states that this report supplies no separate two-copy estimate;
it does not claim no such estimate exists anywhere in the literature.
Genotype letters come from the authorized resolver, not the publications.
The marker, evidence tier and other reports are unchanged. Registration of
these four strings is not complete-catalog or release acceptance.
