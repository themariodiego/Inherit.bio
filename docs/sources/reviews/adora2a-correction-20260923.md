# ADORA2A report correction

Applies to `caffeine-sleep-adora2a-rs5751876`. Primary-source review on
23 September 2026; agent review, not clinical or human signoff.

[Rétey 2007, full paper](https://www.pharma.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:00000000-2992-0cf5-0000-000020c5cb44/Retey_Clin_Pharmacol_Ther_2007.pdf):
Results, Table 1 and Methods, printed pages 693–697, including visual table
inspection. Among the self-rated sensitive/insensitive groups, CC counts were
23/58 and 22/84; TT counts were 4/58 and 16/84. CT frequencies were similar.
Figure 4's intermediate CT finding concerns a particular sleep-EEG response,
not a general anxiety/sleep ranking. Methods explicitly identify rs5751876.

[Alsene 2003, author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12825092/):
94 healthy infrequent users, one 150 mg dose versus placebo, with greater
reported anxiety in TT than the other groups. This supports the qualified
anxiety clause, not an additive CT ranking or a personal caffeine limit.
This correction does not claim full-paper access to Alsene.

The report separates the endpoints, removes the unsupported combined ranking
and CYP1A2 modifier, and retains the existing evidence tier. These findings
do not establish an individual prediction. The prior batch reviews remain
historical access records; this note resolves their sleep-direction question.
The four corrected strings are registered against these two publications.
This remains a limited report correction; complete catalog review and
release-row completion are not claimed.
