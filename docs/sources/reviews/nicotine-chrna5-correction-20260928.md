# Nicotine dependence CHRNA5 report: citation corrected to the paper it names

Applies to `nicotine-dependence-chrna5-rs16969968`. Source review on
28 September 2026; agent review, not clinical or human signoff.

## The defect

The report cited PMID `17158188` under the label "Saccone et al., Hum Mol
Genet 2007". PMID 17158188 is **Bierut et al., 2007**, "Novel genes identified
in a high-density genome wide association study for nicotine dependence", whose
abstract nominates NRXN1 and the beta3 nicotinic receptor and does not mention
CHRNA5 or rs16969968. The label and the identifier named different papers, and
the identifier did not support the report's CHRNA5 finding.

## Sources actually read (28 September 2026)

- [Saccone et al., Hum Mol Genet 2007, PMID 17135278](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17135278/):
  author abstract read through the indexed PubMed record. "Cholinergic
  nicotinic receptor genes implicated in a nicotine dependence association
  study targeting 348 candidate genes with 3713 SNPs." The abstract states the
  most compelling evidence for a risk variant came from the non-synonymous
  CHRNA5 SNP rs16969968. This is the paper the label names, and it supports the
  report's core statement that rs16969968 in CHRNA5 is a replicated
  nicotine-dependence risk variant.
- The report's second citation, [Thorgeirsson et al., Nature 2008, PMID 18385739](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18385739/),
  was retrieved on 28 September and supports the per-cigarette-per-day effect at
  the 15q25 nicotinic cluster; it is unchanged.
- Supporting the allele direction, [Saccone et al., PLoS Genet 2010, PMID 20700436](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20700436/)
  full text (PMC) reports rs16969968 (D398N) with an ordinal odds ratio for
  cigarettes per day rising 1.15, 1.29, 1.40 across smoking categories, and
  [Bierut et al., Am J Psychiatry 2008, PMID 18519524](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18519524/)
  reports the risk allele decreases the receptor's response to a nicotine
  agonist. Ensembl gives rs16969968 as G/A on the forward strand with the A
  (Asn398) allele at 0.37 in Europeans and near-absent in African and East
  Asian panels, matching the report's A = higher-risk direction.

## What changed

Only the first citation's identifier: `17158188` becomes `17135278`, keeping
the "Saccone et al., Hum Mol Genet 2007" label. No report-body wording changed,
because the summary and the three genotype explanations were already supported
by the corrected source and by Thorgeirsson 2008. A saved report therefore
shows no corrected wording; the correction is a source reference, not a finding.

This is a limited citation correction. It does not claim full-catalogue review,
a personal prediction, or clinical validity.
