# Dataset license audit

Every public dataset Inherit stores, serves derived annotations from, or
ships seed data of — each verified against the source's own terms on
**2026-08-28**, except the rows that carry their own later date. Rule: no non-commercial-licensed source may enter the
reference store or seed data.

| Dataset | License / terms | Verdict | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ClinVar (NCBI) | No formal license; NCBI places no restrictions on use or distribution; attribution requested ("provide attribution to ClinVar as a data source"). US-government-authored content is public domain; submitter records carry no rights transfer, so NCBI grants no affirmative license. | **Use, with attribution** | [ClinVar maintenance & use](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/docs/maintenance_use/), [NCBI policies](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) |
| dbSNP (NCBI) | Same NCBI policy: no NCBI-imposed restrictions; no affirmative grant; rare submitter claims possible. Inherit stores only rsID↔position/allele mappings (facts). | **Use, with attribution** | [NCBI policies](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) |
| gnomAD (Broad) | **CC0 1.0** for primary exome/genome data ("free of restrictions"). Caveat: bundled third-party annotation fields inside gnomAD releases may carry their own licenses — Inherit stores only allele frequencies (primary data). | **Use** (attribution as courtesy) | [gnomAD policies](https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org/policies) |
| **gnomAD HGDP + 1kGP harmonised callset** (the sub-continental reference the ancestry model would draw from) | **Read 2026-09-14 from the peer-reviewed paper, because the browser's own policies page could not be read in this environment.** Koenig et al., *A harmonized public resource of deeply sequenced diverse human genomes*, Genome Research 34(5):796 (2024), data-availability section: “All data are freely available”, and of the component projects, “their open sharing policies with consent to release unrestricted individual-level data” and “In addition to unrestricted individual-level public release, we provide detailed tutorials”. **The CC BY 4.0 notice on that article governs the article, not the callset** — the same distinction this table draws for PharmVar, and it is stated here so nobody reads the article licence as the data licence. For the data's own terms the gnomAD row above stands: **CC0 1.0** for primary exome/genome data, verified 2026-08-28, and an allele frequency is primary data. **What was NOT re-verified, stated so a person can finish it:** https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org/policies is a JavaScript-only page; `curl` returns an 8.9 KB shell and the licence text is in no chunk that could be fetched, and the bundled Chromium does not trust this session's TLS-interception CA, so the page could not be rendered either. Verification was not disabled to get around it. **The non-licence question this row deliberately leaves open:** HGDP sampled small, named, often Indigenous populations, and this model would print those names to a reader. Unrestricted release settles what may be *stored*; it does not settle what may be *said about a person*, and `docs/ancestry-resolution.md` measured the concrete harm — a person the reference set does not contain gets a confident, specific, wrong label, most severely in North Africa and the Middle East. | **Use for allele frequencies, with attribution** (cite Koenig et al. 2024 and gnomAD) — on the CC0 row above plus the paper's unrestricted-release statement. Re-read the policies page when a person can, and record the result here. The naming question is a product decision and is tracked separately; a licence verdict is not permission to print a population's name. | [Koenig et al. 2024 (PMC9900804)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9900804/), [gnomAD HGDP+1kGP downloads](https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org/downloads#v3-hgdp-1kg), [gnomAD policies](https://gnomad.broadinstitute.org/policies) |

| PhyloTree (mtDNA tree, Build 17) | **The two records disagreed and ours was the wrong one (D-022, resolved 2026-09-13).** `data/ref/haplogroups/PROVENANCE.md` asserted "free for academic/non-commercial use with citation"; the publisher's own page, read 2026-09-03, states no copyright, licence or terms of use at all and asks only that the 2009 paper be cited. **The non-commercial restriction was ours, not the publisher's**, so the audit's standing bar on non-commercially-licensed sources was disqualifying this source on the strength of a term nobody imposed. The provenance note now says what the page says. Stated precisely, because it is the whole of what changed: data published with no terms is not data published permissively — this is a correction to a claim about what the page says, not counsel's opinion on what rights attach to data published without a licence, and it remains open to counsel to revisit. The shipped topology and defining positions were read from an MIT-licensed machine-readable copy, which licenses that copy's packaging, not the underlying tree. | **Use** — cleared of the non-commercial bar 2026-09-13; see the verdict cell | [PhyloTree](https://www.phylotree.org/), [`data/ref/haplogroups/PROVENANCE.md`](../data/ref/haplogroups/PROVENANCE.md) |

Report-template citations reference the primary literature directly
(PubMed/DOI), not aggregator databases, so no template content derives from
