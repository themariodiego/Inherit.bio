## `docs/route-register.json`

1. `policyResolvers.scope-derived-v1.cases.subject:adult.principalRelationshipCases.uploader-or-grantee`
   - **Old:** no key `builtProjections` (the case ended at `conjunctionAndRevisionBinding`).
   - **New:** key `builtProjections`: built on a local provider only, for two relationships and the reports projection only. Uploader: a confirmed Path B `other_adult` subject the acting account owns whose `subject_account_id` is the person's own account. Grantee: another account's own `self` subject whose person the acting account reads through a current `family_member` relationship (the Family graph's `dataSubjectId`). In order: `other-adult-mitigation-state-v1` at `model-context-before-retrieval` for an `other_adult` subject (the resolver has no case for a `self` subject and the Family recipient reader has never asked it); then `copilot.local` signed by the exact current adult subject principal to this recipient over the current `uploader` or `family_member` relationship and revision; then each report layer's own purpose, read through that relationship's existing recipient reader (saved reports only). The ancestry and variants projections are not built and no tool reads them, although this case permits them with their further grants. Every turn pair records the subject in `retrieved_subject_ids` and `copilot.local` plus each layer read in `retrieved_purpose_keys`, with one `copilot_turn_dependencies` row per subject, grant and source. `cloudProvider` stays `absolute-deny`.
   - **Why:** the "reports only in this version" limit and the two relationships as built; the case's existing keys (`all`, `providerClass`, `requiredLiveCopilotPurpose`, `requiredLivePurposesByProjection`, `conjunctionAndRevisionBinding`) are unchanged.

2. `routes[copilot.scope].policy.pageBranches`
   - **Old:** two branches, `available` and `true-non-self-unavailable`.
   - **New:** a third, `subject-adult-uploader-or-grantee`: for an `s-` segment whose subject the acting account uploaded or holds as a grantee, and only on the available transport, render exactly one of, in order: jurisdiction-unavailable (`jurisdiction-v1` copy for `third_party_adult_analysis`); provider-required (own provider not classified local, or no current Copilot permission); held (`other-adult-mitigation-state-v1` denies with a gate other than not-found; no reason or gate shown); permission-missing (no current `copilot.local` to the acting account or no shared report layer, without saying which); the Tier-2 acknowledgement gate; then the authorized chat surface, whose h1 thread header names the one subject, minting one context token bound to the subject, the provider authority and the exact relationship, Copilot and layer grant revisions. Any other or unrelated subject is `resource-not-found-page-v1`; off the available transport a resolved uploader-or-grantee subject renders `true-non-self-unavailable`.
   - **Why:** the page branch as built. The two existing branches are unchanged.

3. `routes[files.index].policy`
   - **Old:** `contracts` only.
   - **New:** key `pathBCopilotChoice`: the person a Path B file was added for, holding the record with their own account, turns `copilot.local` on and off for the uploader only, beside their result-layer choices on this page (no "for you" Copilot row). Turning it on is `api.consents` `grant-purpose` under the same server-sealed Path B presentation, `consent.share-with-adult` and four statements as a result layer for the uploader, with `other-adult-mitigation-state-v1` at `purpose-grant-create`, signed by the person's own current account principal over the current uploader relationship (`api.consents.signerCases.other-adult-subject-artifact-or-purpose`). Turning it off is `api.consent-revoke` with its one-time `consent_revoke` value and is `purpose-revocation-v1`. The no-account person's rights-session door still refuses `copilot.local`, because no reader exists for a no-account record. The uploader's line for a ready shared report links to `copilot.scope` for that record only while its `subject-adult-uploader-or-grantee` branch would render the chat surface.
   - **Why:** the decision's "permission route" for the uploader case. `api.consents` already lists `copilot.local` as a `grant-purpose` key and already names the signer case, so it needs no change.

Not amended (already describes what is built): `family.person.policy.individualResultPurposeLayers.copilot-local-action: copilot.local` (the entry link on the Family person page); `api.chat` (its body, forbidden fields and authorization order); `api.chat-history`; `copilot-transport-availability-v1` (`trueNonSelfScopes` already lists `subject:adult-uploader-or-grantee`); `cloud-model-consent-v1` (unchanged absolute deny); the withdrawal routes (`copilot.local` is a directional purpose grant, revoked by `revoke_directional_purpose_with_nonce_v1`).
