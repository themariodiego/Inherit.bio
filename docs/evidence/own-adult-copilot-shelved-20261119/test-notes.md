## 2026-11-19 — Copilot for an adult's own record (G8.5, `scope-derived-v1` `subject:adult` `own-subject`)

The owner decided to build Copilot for an `other_adult` record the person holds in their own account, for local models and approved cloud providers, on the terms the signed register already states ("G8.5: Copilot for an adult's own record is built"). Until now `public.own_report_context_v1` admitted only a `self` subject, so the own Copilot permission, authority and chat answered such a record not-found. The guarded migration `20261119100000_own_adult_copilot.sql` adds `private.own_copilot_context_v1`. For a self subject it returns exactly `own_report_context_v1`. For an `other_adult` record it applies the same checks to the record itself, through `own_upload_store_authority_v1`: a live session of the account, an adult and unbanned account, the record's binding and principal, and the record's own `consent.upload-self` and insurance acknowledgement. It also decides, from database rows only, that the record is the person's own: the acting account holds it, no account owns it, it is active, the account holds the `adult_claim` binding and active principal that only Path A's account acceptance writes, the invitation was accepted, and the person's own `consent.subject-adult` signature carries an unrevoked `adult_source` consent. A public appeal holding the record denies every read, as `other_adult_mitigation_v1` denies it first. Three bodies are patched in place under the md5-guarded convention:
- `private.own_copilot_configuration_v1` resolves its context through the new function.
- `private.own_report_purge_scope_v1` admits a Copilot grant on such a record. Without this, the revocation trigger refuses and nothing could be withdrawn.
- `private.purge_path_a_subject_v1` deletes the record's conversations, with their model working state, as `empty_self_subject_v1` does for a self record.

In the application, `copilotSubjectScope`, the own chat's preparation and route, `ownCopilotProviderStatus` and the permission view admit the same shape: held by the account, owned by none. Settings lists one permission section per such record whose permission can be given, after the self record's, which is unchanged. A record the account uploaded or was granted, another account's own record, a record bound to the account but owned by an uploader, embryos, cohorts and every inactive or unbound record stay refused, and so does the uploader-or-grantee case.
