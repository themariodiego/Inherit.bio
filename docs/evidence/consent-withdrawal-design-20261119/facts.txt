## 2026-11-19 — G8.5: a withdrawal is built for three kinds of consent

Owner decision (clickable chat answer, "Build withdrawal for each"). Asked: three kinds of consent can only be withdrawn today by deleting things: a person's consent to upload their own file (deleting a file leaves the consent on record; only emptying the whole record or deleting the account ends it); an uploader's consent for another adult's file (only by deleting that adult's whole record, which also ends that adult's own confirmation); and a non-parent uploader's consent for an embryo upload (only by deleting their account). The design, including what withdrawing an uploader's consent does to the other adult's record and confirmation, comes back to the owner before it is built.
