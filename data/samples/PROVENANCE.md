Public reference materials only. No real individual's private genome is or may
ever be committed to this repository.

  <https://ftp-trace.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ReferenceSamples/giab/release/NA12878_HG001/NISTv4.2.1/GRCh38/>

  `93bc4c2c696eaf13515ab058caecc064bfed704f85bac7482330ca91bc730daa`

  chr20, chr21, chr22 (187,130 variant records); recompressed with gzip.
  Reproduce:
  `zcat HG001_...benchmark.vcf.gz | awk '/^#/ {print; next} $1=="chr20"||$1=="chr21"||$1=="chr22" {print}' | gzip -9`

## synthetic_23andme.txt

- Repository SHA-256:
  `aa2cc50864d2447ff0ff8b32b7963142fddc122b15d3a5b7d9bc06673a84f1db`.

- Fully synthetic file in 23andMe v5 text format, generated by
  `scripts/generate-synthetic-sample.ts` (deterministic, seeded). Genotypes
  are sampled per-variant from the report-template seed data plus filler
  rsIDs; they belong to no person. GRCh37 coordinates, matching real
  23andMe export conventions, so the liftover path is exercised.
